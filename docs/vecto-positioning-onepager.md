# VECTO — Positioning One-Pager

> Source of truth for every piece of outbound copy: reels, DMs, landing pages,
> Hermes reply templates, sales calls. If it contradicts this doc, it's wrong.

---

## 1. Who this is for (ICP)

The **independent freelance personal trainer in India**:

- 2 to 15 active 1:1 clients (not a gym floor trainer).
- Earns ₹40K–₹1.5L/month, tier-1 or tier-2 city (Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Jaipur).
- Client fee ₹7K–₹20K per month per client, paid separately from gym membership.
- 3–8 years of coaching experience. Certified (K11, ACE, ACSM, ISSA) or self-taught with a following.
- Runs the business on WhatsApp + a personal notebook + occasionally Google Sheets.

**Not our ICP (yet):** gym-employed floor trainers, celebrity trainers with a team, online-only trainers with 50+ clients, nutritionists without training clients.

---

## 2. The problem we solve (in the trainer's own words)

> "Clients pay me for 3 months. If results don't show, they drop. No results = no referrals = no testimonials = I'm always hunting new leads."

The real cause of "no results" is almost never the plan. It is **client inconsistency between sessions**. The trainer sees them 4x/week for 45 minutes. The other 165 hours are dark. That is where the plan dies.

---

## 3. The wedge — "Chalta hai kills retention"

Every Indian trainer knows the "chalta hai" client:
- "Bhaiya aaj skip kar dete hain."
- "Yaar biryani thi party mein, kya karein."
- "Diet log karna bhool gaya, but light kha raha tha, promise."

Vecto's job is to make "chalta hai" **visibly, mathematically, undeniably wrong** — every single day, on the client's own phone, before the trainer even has to say anything.

---

## 4. The promise — "The mirror doesn't lie"

Vecto is a binary tracking calendar. Green means logged. Red means missed. There is no "kind of." There is no backdating. The client either did it today, or they didn't. Over 30 days, the calendar becomes an argument the trainer no longer has to make.

**Tagline options** (pick per surface):
- Long: *Whatever gets tracked, gets done.*
- Short: *Direction over motivation.*
- Aggressive: *The mirror doesn't lie.*
- Trainer-facing: *Your clients pay for results. Vecto forces the reps.*

---

## 5. Three proof pillars

**Pillar 1 — Binary Accountability.** No colours between green and red. No "log yesterday's meal today." The client shows up or they don't, and both of you see it. This ends the daily excuse economy.

**Pillar 2 — Zero-Motivation Design.** Logging takes 8 seconds. Photo → AI → done. Weight, steps, workout — all one tap. When friction is lower than the "chalta hai" instinct, compliance wins.

**Pillar 3 — Trainer as Commander, Not Babysitter.** The trainer sets the plan once. Vecto does the daily enforcement. The trainer stops chasing clients on WhatsApp for logs and starts having 5-minute weekly reviews with data on the table. Higher status. Less burnout. Retained clients.

## 8. Voice — Elite Jester

Authoritative, clinical, dry, slightly satirical. Think The Economist meets a gym-floor coach who has seen every excuse.

**Do:** short sentences, one idea per line. Data as punchline. Mock the excuse, never the person. Use Hindi phrases where they land ("chalta hai", "yaar", "bhaiya") — sparingly, on purpose.

**Don't:** motivational quote culture, emoji spam, "Hey champion 👋" openers, discount-driven urgency. We are not a Bazaar deal.

## 10. CTA hierarchy (every surface, in this order)

1. **WhatsApp us** — one tap, pre-filled message. Zero form fields. Highest conversion.
2. **Book a 15-min call** — Calendly, only 4 slots/week to signal scarcity.
3. **Sign up free** — self-serve for trainers who don't want a call.

Never lead with "Sign up." Lead with WhatsApp. Trainers convert on human contact.
